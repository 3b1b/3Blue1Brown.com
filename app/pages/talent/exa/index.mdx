## About

Exa's research vision is "Attention Over Everything." How do we attend over the world's 100 trillion tokens, at sub-200ms speed, to find the right information?

To start, we build massive-scale infra to crawl the entire web, train state-of-the-art language models to process it, and design super high performant vector databases to retrieve over it. We're building a search engine unlike the world has ever seen, and power search for Cursor, Cognition, HubSpot, and over 400,000 developers.

Our team is small, talent-dense, and driven by the mission of responsible and complete information infrastructure for humanity. If we can achieve perfect search so that every AI has the highest quality information, then every human will too.
