The Library of Alexandria existed for centuries. It served knowledge to citizens of the world and provided answers to all types of queries far and wide. A librarian, grey and weary, looked after the scrolls, and loved each and every one equally.

In 48 BCE, there was one final query delivered to the Library of Alexandria. It was a warning…

> The library is soon to perish. Read only the most important words from your scrolls and use them to inscribe and deliver one message to the world. Enemies will find you. They must think your message is nonsense at first glance. Know how importance is reckoned: a word is important if important words stand near it. You can hold eighteen words in mind at once; two words within that span are joined. Joined or not joined. Cast out words that are just connectors before you begin. When the reckoning settles, save the eighteen worth most, and speak them in the order the scroll first spoke them.

And that's all we knew, until, on June 30th, 2021, the librarian's answer was finally found. One scroll of eighteen lines, found in front of a sign: ΕΞΑ.
