"We look for π-shaped people", they told me. That is, rather than just looking for a T-shape of skillsets, generally broad with one deep expertise, they seek people with multiple areas of expertise. The obvious joke is that this aligns with 3Blue1Brown because of the pi creature mascot, but I actually _do_ think this is a meaningful positive signal for people in this audience.

Many of you are broadly technically curious people, and part of being curious like that means engaging with more than just one domain. From all the people I talked to at Kiso, a unifying theme for why they enjoyed working there was the opportunity to work on many different kinds of projects. If you feel π-shaped, they will appeal to you.

Kiso wasn't founded with the intention of being a consultancy, but the founders explained to me how they realized how much you can do today with a small talented team that would have been impossible a few years ago, and the model just fit. One noteworthy feature is how everyone shares the same title, "member of technical staff". I got the impression from everyone I talked to that they were motivated primarily by building, not by climbing a ladder.
