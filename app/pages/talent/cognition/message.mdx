I visited the nascent Cognition team not too long after it was founded a few years ago. What immediately struck me was the sheer concentration of both talent and hustle. It's filled with former founders and IOI/IMO medalists, everyone speaks highly of all their coworkers, and they're all working incredibly hard. The closest analogy I could think of was that it's the engineering equivalent of a team of athletes training for the Olympics.

I knew about them because a college friend of mine, Russell Kaplan, is their president. Throughout the last decade, Russell has had an uncanny ability to predict the major trends in AI a few years before they become mainstream. He's also one of the most savvy and ambitious people I know, so when he pointed his attention towards this new company, I was intrigued.

I've come back to meet the team a few times over the last year in the context of this partnership with 3b1b. Although it's grown a lot since that first visit (gone are the days of everyone coding away in a single shared house), I was pleased to see that through their growth, the talent concentration remains as dense as ever.
