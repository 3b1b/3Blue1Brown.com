All of you watching this channel know there's a lot more to math than what's typically taught in schools. In fact, this is where some of the most beautiful ideas lie. I'm guessing many of you also recognize that not everyone has the chance to see this more creative, extracurricular side of math. BEAM is all about offering the chance to see that other side to students who otherwise might not get it.

In chatting with their team, one thing that struck me is how significant the long-term relationship with students is. It's not just about a single program that a student attends; they care a lot about seeing them through on a longer path towards a STEM career. Anyone who has had the chance to experience such a long-term relationship with a student knows how deeply meaningful it can be.
