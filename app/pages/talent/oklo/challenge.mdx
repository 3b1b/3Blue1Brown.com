When a neutron triggers fission inside a sphere of fissile material, it releases $\nu$ new neutrons that each begin their own random walks — a branching diffusion process. Whether the chain reaction sustains itself or dies out depends entirely on the size of the sphere.

<Figure image={criticalRadius} />

The sphere is **critical** when the expected neutron population stays constant across generations. For a uniform fissile sphere with neutron mean free path $\lambda$ and average neutrons released per fission $\nu$, find the critical radius $R_c$ — the minimum radius at which a self-sustaining chain reaction is possible.
