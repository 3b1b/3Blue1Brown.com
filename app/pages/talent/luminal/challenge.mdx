This take-home exercise is for candidates joining the compiler engineering
team at Luminal. It evaluates instruction scheduling, scratch allocation,
correctness, and engineering judgment when building a compiler backend.

Build a general backend compiler for a small, deterministic VLIW machine. The
input is a typed, straight-line SSA program. Your compiler must assign every
virtual value to the machine's scratchpad and schedule every operation into
VLIW bundles. Correctness is required; shorter schedules and smaller scratch
footprints score better.
