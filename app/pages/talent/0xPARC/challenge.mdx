A lot of pure math problems, often the kind that would arise in a contest math
setting, are very relevant to the work we do.

Suppose I have a secret list of positive integers $(v_1, v_2, ..., v_n)$. You
can't see my list, but you can ask me dot product questions: give me any list
$(x_1, x_2, ..., x_n)$ of the same length, and I'll tell you $x_1 \cdot v_1 +
... + x_n \cdot v_n$. Figure out my secret list using as few questions as
possible.

This one has a surprising connection to lattice-based cryptography and fully
homomorphic encryption.
